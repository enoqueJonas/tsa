# Lesson Map Status

The runtime now represents the planned curriculum down to lesson titles across the full Technical Stewardship Journey.

During the current **build-wide** phase, a planned lesson may intentionally have `activities: []`. This means the lesson belongs to the approved curriculum map but its full TSA-authored teaching content, resources, exercises, labs, knowledge checks, and review criteria have not yet been written.

Existing authored Engineering Foundations lessons remain real lessons with activities. Planned lessons must not be presented as completed lesson content merely because their titles exist in the runtime.

The next breadth steps are to expose the Journey → School → Module → Lesson hierarchy in the product and then add any remaining first-class curriculum concepts needed for labs, milestones, capabilities, portfolio artifacts, and handbook entries before the deep-authoring phase begins.
